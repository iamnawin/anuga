At pilot density (15 families ≈ 150 visits/month across 4–5 companions ≈ 30–38 visits each) a salaried companion costs ≈ ₹691 per visit. **The pilot must use per-visit pay with part-time companions.** A 3-trips/day average (about 78 visits/month) is a scale-stage requirement.

## 6. Break-even: formula, inclusions and cost bases

### 6d. Dependence on late-cancel fee income (v2_base, 40 families)

| Case | CM/visit ₹ |
|---|---|
| 0% of late-cancel fees collected | 170 |
| 35% of late-cancel fees collected | 183 |
| 70% of late-cancel fees collected | 197 |

A late cancel earns more than it costs, so a higher cancellation rate looks 'profitable' in the tornado. That is an artefact of a strict policy that elderly families are unlikely to accept; do not bank on it. Use the 0% row as the prudent view.
