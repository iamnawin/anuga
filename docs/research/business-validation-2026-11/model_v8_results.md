# v8: ideal patient, Safe Return tier, expansion hypotheses

## 1. Is the service worth it for the family? Time value of the escort

Family member's time freed per trip: 7 hours (about 3 travel/handover + 4 waiting) `[E]`; 12 trips = 84 hours. The cab or fuel cost is paid either way, so the **incremental** cost of AnugaCare is the service fee, not the fare. Value only counts if the time is real (unpaid leave, lost work, or a family member who would otherwise be free).

| Family member's annual income | Value per hour ₹ (2,000 h/year) | Value of 84 hours ₹ | Plan fee ₹ | Value ÷ fee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ₹3 L | 150 | 12,600 | 10,999 | 1.1× |
| ₹6 L | 300 | 25,200 | 10,999 | 2.3× |
| ₹12 L | 600 | 50,400 | 10,999 | 4.6× |
| ₹24 L | 1,200 | 100,800 | 10,999 | 9.2× |

Break-even: the plan pays for itself in time value if the family member's time is worth more than about ₹131 per hour, roughly ₹2.6 L a year.

## 2. Ideal patient scorecard (for screening interviews and the deposit test)

| Signal | Fits | Does not fit |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency | Three times a week (12 to 13 trips) | Twice a week or less |
| Age and frailty | 60+, or weak and dizzy after sessions, or uses a stick or wheelchair | Fit, independent, travels alone happily |
| Who escorts today | A working child or spouse who loses a day, or no one reliable | A retired relative with free time |
| Session fee | ₹3,500+ per session, private or corporate centre | Free scheme (white card), or tight budget at ₹2,500 |
| Geography | Within about 8 km of 2 to 3 target centres, in one micro-market | Over 15 km or scattered |
| Spending today | Pays an attendant ₹1,000+ a day or books cabs for every trip | Uses free bus or walks |
| Care need | Non-clinical help only | Needs clinical monitoring in transit (ambulance case) |

## 3. Patients who come and go alone: a lighter tier

Many patients settle in and manage; the risky part is the return home after the session (fatigue and low blood pressure, recovery median about 4 hours in one study `[V]`). A one-way **Safe Return** (meet at the centre, ride home, hand to family) costs less companion time than a round trip.

| Tier | Price | Companion pay ₹ | Contribution per trip (40 families) | Per month at 12 trips |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Safe Return ₹549 per trip, 12 trips = ₹6,499 | 542 per trip | 260 | 134 | 1,604 |
| Safe Return ₹649 per trip, 12 trips = ₹7,799 | 650 per trip | 260 | 243 | 2,922 |
| Door-to-Door plan ₹10,999 (S1) | 917 per trip | 430 | 334 | 4,008 |

Safe Return pay is `[E]`: about half of the round-trip effort. It is a feeder for families not yet ready for ₹10,999, and a way to serve solo travellers.

Reading: dialysis has the best mix of frequency, tenure and predictable schedule. Chemo adds high-value, low-frequency use; radiotherapy and physiotherapy add volume but short tenure, which raises acquisition cost per rupee earned. Expansion should follow a working dialysis model in one micro-market, not run in parallel.
