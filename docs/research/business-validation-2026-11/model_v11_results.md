### Trial terms that stay inside what you control

- Six round trips within 14 days, booked on a fixed weekly pattern; trips start within a stated window after booking.
- A named companion is confirmed 48 hours before each trip. If we cannot cover a trip we tell you at least 12 hours ahead where possible, refund it and add a ₹150 credit. **We do not promise a replacement companion** until measured replacement times exist.
- Cancel up to 12 hours before a trip at no charge. Stop at any time; unused trips are refunded within 7 days.
- The ₹500 deposit, if paid, is credited to the trial.

## B. Cost floors for other service categories (illustrative, NOT dialysis economics)

Method: floor = [companion hours × loaded rate × uplift + commute + per-visit costs] ÷ (1 − payment fee), plus a failure allowance. Loaded rate ₹110 per hour `[E]` (from ₹18,000 to ₹22,000 a month plus on-costs, against a Telangana ward-boy minimum wage of ₹15,960 `[V]`). Per-visit costs: insurance, support, messaging; categories with higher risk carry more. No overhead, founder pay, acquisition cost or margin is included, so a floor is the lowest price that avoids a direct loss, never a target price. Pass-through items (vehicle, tickets, meals) are excluded.

| Category | Hours | Companions | Night uplift | Companion cost ₹ | Commute ₹ | Insurance ₹ | Support ₹ | Messaging ₹ | Failure allowance | **Cost floor ₹** | Floor per companion-hour ₹ | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short visit (2 h, home or nearby) | 2 | 1 | 1 | 220 | 60 | 15 | 30 | 8 | 5% | **358** | 179 | walk, conversation, errands, reading, tech help |
| Long visit (4 h) | 4 | 1 | 1 | 440 | 60 | 25 | 40 | 8 | 5% | **616** | 154 | longer company, meals, supervised activities at home |
| Day outing (8 h door to door) | 8 | 1 | 1 | 880 | 60 | 60 | 60 | 15 | 7% | **1,178** | 147 | temple, shopping, family function, cinema; vehicle and tickets pass-through |
| Day outing, high support (wheelchair, 2 companions) | 8 | 2 | 1 | 1,760 | 120 | 90 | 80 | 15 | 7% | **2,263** | 141 | mobility aids; two people |
| Overnight (12 h, home or hospital bystander) | 12 | 1 | 1.25 | 1,650 | 100 | 80 | 100 | 15 | 7% | **2,131** | 178 | night uplift 1.25 `[E]`; statutory overtime and night rules `[I]` |

Floors are the outputs of the formula and nothing more. Commute, insurance and support figures for the longer categories are my guesses for higher risk and effort.

Use of anchors: they show that someone has priced short companionship at about ₹400 to ₹500 an hour. They do not show demand in Hyderabad, cost to serve, or that AnugaCare can earn a margin there. Every category needs its own paid micro-pilot before any price is published.
