# v9: companion pay, funnel and gig-fee maths

## 1. What does ₹430 per trip mean as an hourly rate?

| Basis | Hours | ₹ per hour at ₹430 | ₹ per hour at ₹390 (own two-wheeler) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Active time (travel, rides, handovers) | 3.6 | 119 | 108 |
| Elapsed time (first pickup to second drop; includes the 3 h gap between legs) | 6.5 | 66 | 60 |

Benchmarks `[V]` unless noted: Telangana ward-boy minimum wage ₹15,960 a month from 1 Apr 2026 (about ₹77 an hour on 26 days × 8 h); Hyderabad ward-boy and patient-care job listings ₹16,000 to ₹24,000 (some ₹18,000 to ₹30,000), about ₹77 to ₹144 an hour; HCAH attendant ₹1,000+ a day; Rapido's own target of ₹70 to ₹80 an hour net after fuel for two-wheeler riders (company statement). Rapido and Uber earnings claims (₹65,000 to ₹100,000 a month per Rapido; older cab study ₹45,000 to ₹50,000 gross, about ₹10,000 net after EMI) are unverified and conflict.

The gap between legs is only useful if the companion can fill it. A pickup due at 10:30 means no new ride after about 9:45. Two staggered patients a day make the gap productive.

At 37 trips (₹15,910) a guarantee of ₹8,000 never binds. It only matters in weak months, early weeks, and for companions on fewer than about 19 trips. Offer it after 20 completed trips.
