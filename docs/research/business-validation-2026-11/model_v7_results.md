# v7: companion with an own vehicle

Distance per round trip: 16 km with the patient plus about 24 km of companion solo legs (to the home, away, back to the centre, away) = about 40 km `[E]`. Operating cost per km (fuel, tyres, servicing, insurance, depreciation) `[E]`: two-wheeler ₹3, CNG auto ₹4.5, hatchback ₹11.

## 1. Vehicle cost per trip and what it does to delivered cost

| Vehicle | Operating cost per km ₹ | Cost for 40 km ₹ | Can carry the patient? | Companion time pay ₹ | Total delivered cost ₹ | Baseline: cab ₹388 + pay ₹430 = ₹818 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two-wheeler (companion's own legs only) | 3 | 72 (solo legs only) | No (pillion unsafe post-dialysis); cab still needed | 330 | 790 | saves about 28 |
| CNG auto, commercial permit | 4.5 | 180 | Yes, but basic comfort | 330 | 510 | saves about 308 |
| Hatchback, commercial permit | 11 | 440 | Yes | 330 | 770 | saves about 48 |

Deadhead kilometres are the problem: only 16 of about 40 km are billable patient distance, so a car saves almost nothing against an aggregator cab plus a ₹60 commute allowance. Only a cheap-to-run vehicle (auto, bike for the solo legs) saves money.

## 2. Contribution per trip, S1 plan (₹917), 40 families

| Case | Companion effective pay ₹ | Customer fare per round trip ₹ | Contribution per trip ₹ | Customer saves per month ₹ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline: no vehicle, aggregator cab | 430 | 388 | 334 | 0 |
| Companion owns a two-wheeler (commute allowance ₹60 → ₹20) | 390 | 388 | 376 | 0 |
| Companion owns a CNG auto, patient pays meter fare | 330 | 239 | 440 | 1,788 |

In the auto case the companion also keeps the fare (₹239) against about ₹180 running cost, so their take is about ₹330 + ₹59 = ₹389 per trip, a little below the baseline ₹430 + commute, which is why the pay is a judgement and may need to be higher.
