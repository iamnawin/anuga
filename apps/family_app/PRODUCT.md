# Family and patient views

AnugaCare supports families and elderly patients arranging scheduled dialysis
visits. This package supplies the Family widgets mounted by the unified app.
The database remains authoritative for every access level and mutation.

Calm, readable product UI: existing teal/mint/navy light and dark themes, system
font with Telugu/Hindi fallback, 48px targets, 56px primary buttons, 16-18px copy,
and full 200% text scaling. Use existing panels and neutral illustrated avatars
or initials. No photos, maps, ETA, badges or companion phone numbers.

Binding design: Family_Screens_FINAL_Spec_v1.md on research branch
ccr-d6e3aaf3-jvijtn; existing docs/design/tokens.md. Evidence and contract gaps:
docs/evidence/family-screens-v2/README.md. Static sample data belongs in tests only.
