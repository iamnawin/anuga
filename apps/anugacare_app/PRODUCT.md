# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Older dialysis patients checking today's visit, family coordinators requesting
and following care, non-clinical companions completing assigned visit steps, and
Ops staff confirming, assigning and resolving exceptions. Users commonly work
on small Android screens, in mixed English/Telugu/Hindi contexts and under time
pressure around scheduled treatment.

## Product Purpose

AnugaCare coordinates recurring non-clinical dialysis visits. Success means each
authorized role can understand and complete its next supported action while the
database preserves consent, privacy, OTP, assignment and event-order rules.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dependable and humane. The interface should reduce uncertainty without
promising clinical care, emergency dispatch, live location or availability that
the system cannot prove.

## Anti-references

- Ride-hailing UI that implies on-demand matching, live GPS or automatic ETA.
- Clinical dashboards that make companions appear to provide medical care.
- Decorative healthcare mockups with fictional controls or completion claims.
- Dense generic SaaS cards, tiny text, low-contrast controls or role pickers.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real persisted state and one clear next action.
2. Keep restricted information absent, not merely visually hidden.
3. Prefer familiar Material controls and large readable targets.
4. Make refresh, denial, retry and session recovery explicit.
5. Use the same journey vocabulary across Family and Companion views.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, 48 logical-pixel minimum targets, 56-pixel primary actions,
screen-reader semantics, safe scrolling at 200% text, platform font fallbacks for
Telugu and Devanagari, reduced-motion-safe feedback, and no meaning conveyed by
color alone. Human reviewers approve Telugu/Hindi healthcare/service meaning.

The repository-wide product and access authority remains `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md`.
